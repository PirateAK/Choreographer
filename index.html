Show settings

The length a cue is given when it is placed, before it is dragged into shape. These travel with the show, so a reopened file flies and fades the same way. End fade is how long the music takes to come down into an end marker; 0 stops it dead on the moment. Tempo and counts per bar are the music's clock: a phrase is placed by counts of it, and the bars are drawn under the tracks and counted from where the first track begins.

Running order 1

Acts run end to end on one clock, so Act 2 begins where Act 1 finishes. Set how long each one runs; switching act moves the playhead, not the stage. Drag a scene's notch on the timeline band to move that boundary.

Music none

The tracks are the show's clock. + Track adds one where the playhead is — put the playhead where the music should change, then attach the file. plays trims how much of the file sounds, and end here sets that from the playhead.

Wing legs 0

Legs stand in the wings and split each side into front, middle and back. Drag one on the stage, or type its depth.

Lights 0

A light hangs over a spot on the floor, washes a pool of its own colour and is cued up and out from its own track. Drag its marker on the plan to place it; hold Alt to ignore the grid.

Cast 0

Dancers are movement tracks. Groups (such as Audience) are organizational cast entries for now, not stage objects. Ctrl-click an eye to keep more than one dancer on stage.

Overlaps —

Two bodies closer than 0.55 m — the same test the run-through stops on, but read across the whole show instead of only where the clock happens to run. A fix moves the least it can and re-checks the feet it might pull: it is a normal edit, so Undo puts the path back exactly as it was.

Green room 0

A costume is colours and a cut — body, skirt, hair. Dress a performer with the picker on their row in Performers, and the plan, the elevation and the run-through all wear it.

Props 0

Drag props on the stage; hold Alt to ignore grid snapping.

Movements

Click a move to put it on the point being edited, then close this and look at the elevation. A position and a step belong to a stage station; a level to how that station meets the floor. Or choose a phrase at the top and place the whole step at once.

About Choreographer

A theatre planner for shaping a show, one cue at a time.

Version 0.4.6 Release date: 5 November 2026

Plan and Elevation are separate tabs, so the plan takes the whole stage area when shown. Stage controls sit on a dedicated toolbar row; Tilt view becomes Reset while active.

Legs and feet preview: shaped thighs/calves and articulated heel, ball and toe rendering. Movement technique remains under review.

Includes Mirror path and Cast tools. Show-video recording is disabled; music and voice recording remain available.

Show files are JSON; music and voice recordings are stored separately in this browser. Keep a copy of your show file when moving between browsers or computers.

School conventions

Defaults are the current best case. Enter the school's value to change a position; clear it to return to the default.

Applied rows change the drawing now. Recorded rows are the school's words for a position the solver does not read yet; they wait until that position is re-solved, and the table does not pretend otherwise.
